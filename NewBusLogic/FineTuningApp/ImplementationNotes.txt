## REQUIREMENTS FROM LOGICALTWEEKS.TXT

### 1. Financial Summary Display Order on "My Funds" Screen
The following order should be used:
1. Balance / Balance Due
2. Expected Contribution
3. Total Contribution
4. Outstanding Amount (Outstanding Contributions + Penalties)
5. Outstanding Contributions
6. Penalties

### 2. Excel Data Verification
- Excel file: "Peoples Liberator Fund Contributions 30 June 2025 26-2-16 FINAL.xlsx"
- Check if all relevant and required data is imported to the database
- Focus on "2024-2025" sheet for final totals
- Data should be imported until and including June 2025

### 3. Key Data Points to Verify
1. Expected Contribution - Already worked out
2. Actual Contributions = What members actually paid
3. Balance brought forward = closing balance from the previous year
4. Estimated 12 months contribution = column C + column D
5. Total contribution for 12 months = column BK (O+S+W+AA+AE+AI+AQ+AM+AU+AY+BC+BG)
6. Closing Balance = Total outstanding (H) + Penalty for the year (L) + Balance Brought Forward (E)
7. Penalty for the year (L) = R+V+Z+AD+AH+AL+AP+AT+AX+BB+BF+BJ
8. Capped penalties = Penalties capped from 2018 until 2024 Nov
9. Penalties**: 5.5% monthly interest for late payments (balance brought forward + current month contribution x 5.5%). Penalty for late payment in the next month is amount due + current month contribution x 5.5%)
10. Total fund contributions = all contribution made by members
